# UNO Fandom line: per-deck special cards

> Reference spec for `docs/gameversions.md` (see its Part F). Unlike files 01-06, this pass read primary Mattel sources directly. Those sources are the instruction sheets on service.mattel.com and the product copy on creations.mattel.com and shop.mattel.com. Where no sheet exists, the box-back text in Mattel's own product photos was used.

Method and confidence:
- **HIGH (sheet CODE)**: exact text read from Mattel's instruction sheet PDF.
- **MED (MC copy)**: Mattel Creations or Mattel Shop product description, which is official but only a summary.
- **MED (box)**: box-back blurb read from Mattel's own product photo. It gives the official rule name and a one-line gist, but not the full rule text.
- **LOW**: inferred.

---

## The line

UNO Fandom is Mattel's premium "collectible deck" line, launched on Mattel Creations on **2024-03-20**. The first wave was:
- Monster High HYH54
- Masters of the Universe HYH55
- the four Harry Potter house decks JBD82-85
- Star Trek TOS JBD86
- Avatar JBD87
- six NFL teams HYH48-53

Later waves:
- 26 more NFL teams, JDK00-25 (2024-09-04)
- three Batman 85th-anniversary decks, JDJ97-99 (2024-09-19)
- four Walmart-exclusive Star Wars decks, JCW36-39 (sheets dated 11/2023)
- Super Bowl LIX made-to-order decks, JKB92/93 (2025)
- Monster High Fearbook JFP08 (2025-03)
- Clueless JMD55 (2025-07)
- Harry Potter Dark Arts JLG90 (2025-09)
- Chucky JLG92, Elf JLG93 and Gremlins JLG94 (2025-10)
- She-Ra JFP10 (2026-02)
- Ken 65th JFP09 (2026-03)
- Nightmare Before Christmas JNV41 (2026-09)
- A Charlie Brown Christmas JNV43 (2026-09)

**Format (sheet-verified on every sheet found):**
- The box holds "113 Cards – including a Special Rule card". That is 112 playing cards plus 1 extra **foil** card.
- Mattel's copy says "Includes 112 premium-quality cards … and an extra alt-art foil card".
- Each deck adds **one themed Wild with a special rule**. The rest is a standard 108-card core: 76 numbers, 8 each of Skip, Reverse and Draw Two, 4 Wild and 4 Wild Draw Four.
- Every sheet has a "Special Foil Card" paragraph: the foil is "an alternate version" (Gremlins and Dark Side say "a duplicate") of the deck's special Wild. "You may keep it separate as a collectible or use it in game play … If you use it, be sure to take the other [special] Card out of the deck." **The foil is a swap-in, never an extra copy in play.**
- The sheets use the newer rule text throughout:
  - Draw Two and Wild Draw Four use the "challenge" wording. A wrongly challenged challenger draws 6.
  - UNO is called when you have 1 card, and the penalty for being caught is 2 cards.
  - An Action Card turned up as the first discard is ignored and another card is flipped.
  - Every special Wild scores 50 points.

**Copy count of the special Wild:** none of the sheets lists counts. 112 − 108 = 4, and the scoring lines list only Wild, Wild Draw 4 and the special Wild, so **4 copies** is the consistent reading. Ken JFP09's copy says the rule "comes into play on four of the deck's cards". Treat 4 as MED. The sheets' "take the other … Card out" is loose wording, not evidence of a single copy.

**Exceptions:**
- Ken JFP09 has **no foil**: the box says "Contents: 112" and the copy does not mention a foil.
- NFL HYH50 and JDK16 and Gremlins JLG94 score **number cards at a flat 5 points**, not face value. Other sheets use face value.

## Edge cases & rulings (engine decisions needed)

1. **Foil card.** Treat it as cosmetic: an optional art swap for one copy of the special Wild. It is never a 5th copy. Ken has none.
2. **Out-of-turn plays:**
   - Avatar State may be played "AT ANY TIME (even out of turn!)" if the hand holds all 4 colors. Play then "resumes in current order from you", so the turns of everyone between are lost.
   - Hail Mary interrupts on another player's UNO call. "First come" resolves simultaneous plays, so a digital version needs a reaction window with a tie-break.
   - Browns' Dawg Pound is NOT out of turn ("on your turn"). It needs a "this player called UNO since your last turn" flag.
3. **Hail Mary wording.** "you get to discard down to 1 card" is literal: the Hail Mary player dumps all but one card. This needs a cap or a choice of which card to keep, and the sheet does not say. If the guess is wrong, the color still changes to the named color and play passes to the left of the Hail Mary player.
4. **Gremlins.** "the same unfortunate souls" are the players whose revealed card matched. Each of them draws N more, where N is the total number of matches. Non-matching revealers just keep their 1 card. The player of the card is exempt.
5. **Droid build vs Christmas Tree.** Both are "different color than previous" chains, but they resolve differently:
   - Droids: the chain-breaker draws 3, and **all laid cards return to hands**.
   - Christmas Tree: one lap only, players who can't play are skipped, cards are **removed from hands**, Action cards are inert, a Wild resets the color constraint, and you can't lay your last card.
6. **Rule of Two / Called Shot / Sandy Claws** are hand-transfer effects:
   - Rule of Two: 0-2 cards, chooser's pick, can't give your last card.
   - Called Shot: the target names a color, and if you hold it you give one card of that color.
   - Sandy Claws: a one-lap chain starting with you, and a passed-on card may be the one just received.
   - None of them grants an extra turn.
7. **My Favorite Line / Slytherin hiss / Seahawks / Jaguars / Vikings / Chargers / Bills / Giants** are real-world performance or reaction cards. Implement them as a reaction-race or quiz prompt, or as an honor toggle, the same way as #22 Kevin's Chili.
8. **Batman Bomb.** The player's own hand is untouched. The other hands are pooled and redealt "as evenly as possible". The engine must pick the deal start, so use next-in-order.
9. **Several decks reuse existing engine effects:**
   - Shuffle Hands: Bears. Batman Bomb is a variant that excludes the player.
   - Draw-cancel shields: 49ers, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw, Clueless, Lions, Patriots (Skip only).
   - Discard-pile pick: Ken.
   - Draw-until-color: Jets (green).

   Before building a deck-specific card, map each to its generic effect.
10. **Masters of the Universe codes.** There are three MOTU decks, and they should not be conflated:
    - **GVY91** (©2020, non-Fandom): purple replaces yellow. Its card is the 4× Wild Power of Greyskull (sheet GVY91).
    - **HYH55** (2024 Fandom): "The Armory of Grayskull".
    - **JMF11** (movie deck): Wild Power Sword (sheet JMF11). On play, stand and declare "I have the power!", choose the color and keep the card face-up in front of you. While you hold it you may play up to two cards per turn, and if both are action cards they hit the next player together. You lose it, returning it to the bottom of the discard pile, when another player plays any Wild or their own Power Sword.

    Doc row #330 wrongly gives the Fandom deck code GVY91.
11. **Missing full texts.** Only box blurbs or Mattel Creations copy are known for the following decks:
- MOTU HYH55, Monster High HYH54 and Fearbook JFP08
- the four HP houses and Dark Arts
- Star Trek
- Batman TAS JDJ98 and Classic TV JDJ97
- Elf, Chucky, She-Ra, Clueless and Ken
- 30 of the 32 NFL team decks, plus the Super Bowl LIX decks
