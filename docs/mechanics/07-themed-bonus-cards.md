# 07 — Themed bonus cards: official instruction-sheet check

The reskin pass (Part D RESKIN rows, summarized in A14) built its bonus-card list from web-search
snippets because page fetches were blocked. This pass reads Mattel's own instruction sheets from
service.mattel.com and records what they say. Scope: Mattel-made licensed decks with a bonus card.
UNO Fandom decks are left out because they are covered in `08-fandom-line.md`.

How to read the table:

* **Code** is the Mattel product number whose sheet was read. "(sheet CODE)" in the last column
  means the effect text comes straight from that sheet, so the row is HIGH. A "(scan)" note means
  the sheet is an image-only PDF; it was rendered and read by eye rather than text-extracted.
* **Copies** comes from the sheet's Contents list. Newer one-page sheets only say "112 Cards", and
  108 + 4 = 112, so those rows say "4 (inferred)".
* Unless the row says otherwise, every bonus card is **also a Wild**: the player who plays it picks
  the color that play continues in. If one is turned up as the first discard, the player to the
  dealer's left picks the color. The table leaves this boilerplate out.
* **Primitive** uses the A14 names.
* Rows with no sheet on Mattel's site are still listed, marked **unverified**, so the table is
  complete for the titles covered. For those, the "effect" column repeats the doc's current claim.

## Where the official text differs from the doc's current claim

1. **#251 Coco "Remember Me"** is a memory quiz: the next player must name the two buried discards or draw 3. It is not a hand swap, so A14's "Remember Me = Wild Swap Hands" is wrong.
2. **#82 Lion King "Circle of Life"**: each player passes ONE chosen card left, not their whole hand. A14's "Circle of Life = Wild Pass Hands" is wrong; Disney Parks GPV93 is the whole-hand pass.
3. **#84 Cars 3 "Crash-Up"** triggers when the card is DRAWN from the pile, not when it is played. There is no separate "Smokey" card.
4. **#85 Incredibles 2 "Screen Slayer"**: you put two of YOUR cards into another player's hand while the others' eyes are shut. It does not rearrange cards between two other hands. Primitive: Give cards away.
5. **#83 Infinity Gauntlet**: players who HOLD the chosen color draw 2. The doc says players lacking it draw.
6. **#81 note, Mandalorian "This is the Way"** blocks only a Draw 2 / Wild Draw 4 and redirects it to any other player. It does not block "any action card".
7. **#88 Forces of Nature** lasts until the player's next turn. The sheet adds no special draw penalty.
8. **#91 Superman Returns** has one bonus card, Kryptonite ×4: each other player in turn plays a green card or a Wild, or draws 3. The "two unique wilds" claim is wrong.
9. **#129 Robin's Rule**: the chosen player gets to discard every PINK card. It helps them, and red cards aren't included.
10. **#131 Rad Style**: players WEARING the called color draw 2, not players wearing the wrong color.
11. **#144 Where Everyone Fits In**: a player draws 3 unless they hold MORE of the called icon, and ties draw too.
12. **#156 King Pig**: one round-robin deal from the pile that stops when the FIRST opponent receives the color. It is not "each draws until".
13. **#172 Frozen (CJM70)** contains only Olaf's Summertime ×4. There is no Elsa's Freeze card.
14. **#183 Lightyear "Hyperspeed"**: while the chosen color stays in play, every play of that color skips the next player. Changing the color ends it.
15. **#191 Space Jam "Welcome to the Jam"**: drop the card, and you discard 2 if it lands Slam side up or draw 2 if it lands UNO side up. It affects you, not the next player.
16. **#192 Spirit "Best Friends"**: call a NUMBER, and every player in turn may discard a card of that number, with you last.
17. **#208 Web Swing**: everyone reveals, and you skip ONE player who is showing the named color. You cannot skip several.
18. **#301 Despicable Me 4** is "Mega Minions Power": others discard Mega-Minions-icon action cards and redraw the same number. The 2016 FDV57 card is "Mutated Minion", not "Evil Minion".
19. **#479 Wednesday**: any black Wild (Wild, Wild Draw 4, or Happy Color) answers it. A miss draws 1 and passes the rule on, and it ends if it returns to you.
20. **#437 T. Rex**: a chained reveal that stops at the first player who misses, and backfires on you (draw 5) if every player has the color.
21. **#147 Save the Day** and **#167 Hero Card** come 2 per deck, alongside 2 Wild Customizable cards. #167's card name is "Hero Card".
22. **#135 World Tour**, **#136 Best of Both Worlds**, **#90 Kickin' Battle**, **#75 Crossing the Streams** and **#189 Dr. Blowhole's Revenge** match the doc in substance. The sheet adds detail (see table), and #189 moves from MED-LOW to HIGH.
23. **#293 TMNT (2013)**: CJM71's sheet *is* "Team Attack". The row's warning not to attribute Team Attack to it is wrong.
24. **#315 My Scene** is a real deck (H9022, ©2005, "Casting Call"), so the "doc error" verdict is reversed. **#316 Barbie (2013)** most likely = CHL29 "Barbie Life in the Dreamhouse" (©2013, BFF Power), not HPY59, which means the #316/#424 merge should be revisited.
25. **#149 / #330**: GVY91 is the 2020 non-Fandom MOTU deck, so #330 should not cite this code.
26. **#262 Monster High T8233** is ©2011, not 2013.
27. Smaller gaps: **#177**'s Gryffindor numbers (1-4) are not on the sheet. **#214**'s Cerebro text is from the 2026 JNP43 deck.
